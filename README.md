This entry sums up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in a single newspaper front page. I chose the broadsheet form because it does what a one-day summary needs: the day is named at the top in large type, and six stories share the page on equal footing, so a reader who missed the day can take in its shape at a glance. The six stories span the Middle East, the United States, Europe, the climate talks, and Ukraine, and cover energy and shipping, technology, politics, defence, climate, and war — the day's most consequential threads, each reduced to a headline and one plain factual line, with nothing invented beyond what was reported.
